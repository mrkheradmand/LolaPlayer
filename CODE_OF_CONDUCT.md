# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Lola Player is committed to maintaining a respectful, welcoming, and constructive environment for everyone participating in the project.

We encourage meaningful collaboration, thoughtful feedback, and professional communication.

## Expected Behavior

All community participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Provide constructive and relevant feedback.
- Respect differing opinions, experiences, and technical perspectives.
- Keep discussions focused on Lola Player and its development.
- Report bugs and suggest improvements clearly and respectfully.
- Respect the time and efforts of maintainers and contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Offensive, threatening, or abusive language.
- Trolling, spam, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly engaging in hostile or unproductive discussions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related interactions, including:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- GitHub Discussions
- Other official project communication channels

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to moderate discussions, remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when these standards are violated.

Enforcement decisions will consider the nature, severity, and context of the behavior.

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer privately through an appropriate project communication channel.

Reports will be reviewed with discretion and handled fairly.

## Our Goal

Our goal is to keep Lola Player's community productive, respectful, and focused on building better software together.
